# Varun OS design system

## Product

Varun OS is the portfolio shell for an enterprise product designer. Its job is to help recruiters and design leaders understand the work in 90 seconds, while giving UX practitioners a deeper evidence path. The existing Figma-inspired workspace remains the Work application.

## Experience architecture

Boot → guided Start Here desktop → 90-second flagship case-study route or full Work workspace. Secondary apps expose Case Studies, Process, About, Resume and Contact.

## Visual direction

An operating system for clarity: graphite workspace, warm paper windows, cobalt decisions and fine system-map lines. It is precise, editorial and creative, but never a literal Windows/macOS clone. Use local Geist Sans and Geist Mono only. Use crisp 1px borders, 8–12px radii, minimal shadows and no glassmorphism.

## Hierarchy and readability

The welcome window is the only dominant object. Body text is at least 14px on desktop and 15px on mobile. Primary actions are explicit. Technical labels may be 11–12px. The layout must remain readable at 115% browser zoom.

## Motion

Boot motion is expressive and lasts no more than 2.4 seconds; always include Skip intro. Returning visits and reduced-motion users bypass the long sequence. Steady-state motion is restrained: window reveal, dock selection, connector drift and app transition only.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses an OS canvas, app rail, centered dock and movable welcome window. Mobile becomes a direct app-like home: no tiny draggable desktop, no horizontal overflow, larger tap targets and the same content priority.

## UX and accessibility

Every visual control must have a real outcome. Buttons use native semantics, visible focus, 44px touch targets where practical and keyboard access. Do not depend on color alone. Preserve case-study routes and provide a clear way back from Work to Varun OS.
